\section{Local connection and the input dictionary}
\label{sec:revised11}

Solving metric compatibility and zero torsion gives $\Gamma^{\mathrm{LC}}_{abc}=(C_{abc}+C_{cba}-C_{bac})/2$. The last connection index is the derivative direction. Reconstruct geometric torsion as $T_{abc}=C_{abc}+\Gamma_{acb}-\Gamma_{abc}$. Substitution of (D) gives the same connection in the manuscript and in the code.

For constant coefficients in this noncoordinate frame,
\begin{equation}
R^a{}_{bcd}
=\Gamma^a{}_{ec}\Gamma^e{}_{bd}
-\Gamma^a{}_{ed}\Gamma^e{}_{bc}
+\Gamma^a{}_{be}C^e{}_{cd}.
\tag{CURV}\label{eq:CURV}
\end{equation}
The structure-constant term is essential. Parameter domains transform with their signs: code $V_\tau>0$ means geometric $V<0$, and $\eta_\tau\in[a,b]$ maps to $\eta\in[-b,-a]$. The scale, length, and couplings do not change. Invariance of scalar results under simultaneous sign reversal does not permit discarding the signs of torsion or the three-form.

The spherical scalar $24/r^2$ fixes physical radius $r/2$ and its global volume. The product of the three shape factors is one. Nil uses the domain and gluing in Section 2.
