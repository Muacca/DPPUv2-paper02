\section{Revision note and reference curves}
\label{sec:revised14}

This version uses coefficient $1/2$ in the curvature-side component expression of complete NY and specifies global volumes and the torsion-input dictionary. The curvature identities and Weyl analysis are organized into the fixed-parameter classification of Theorems 1--3. The comparison below isolates the effects on the reference potential. Section 9 states the variational scope and limits of physical interpretation.

The preceding reference inputs were $V_\tau=4$, $\eta_\tau=-2$, $\theta=L=\kappa=1$, corresponding to geometric $V=-4$, $\eta=2$. Equation (REF) and the left panel of Figure~\ref{fig:round_reference} compare the old NY expression with complete NY at the same present volume.

\begin{equation}
U_{\mathrm{old,scaled}}(r)=\frac{4\pi^2}{3}r^2(r-3),
\qquad U_{\mathrm{corrected}}(r)=\frac{4\pi^2}{3}r^3.
\tag{REF}\label{eq:REF}
\end{equation}

The complete-NY curve increases strictly at positive radius, so the old expression's minimum at $r=2$ is absent. Published action values also used a spherical volume eight times the present value; the component correction and the volume change must therefore be distinguished. The preceding toroidal volume was also $2\pi$ times the present one. The right panel shows the four slices in Section 7. Its $\eta_\tau=0$ minimum realizes a different fixed-parameter condition, rather than a replacement physical vacuum for the preceding reference point.
