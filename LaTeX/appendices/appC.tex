\section{Proof details and normalizations}
\label{sec:revised13}

Equation (R) follows by solving the Cartan equations for general $n_i$ and using (CURV). The torsion contribution is $-6\eta^2/r^2-2V^2/3$; no term linear in both $n_i$ and $\eta$ survives.

Equation (W) follows from LC curvature and Ricci norms. Identity (B) uses $1-x^3=-(x-1)(x^2+x+1)$ and is valid for all positive $x$, independently of finite numerical sampling. The derivative of the round polynomial is $3V^2r^2+b$, which yields the classification including its degenerate cases.

The Pontryagin coefficient is defined by exterior products, retaining its factor of two relative to the all-component sum. Zero examples alone cannot check a normalization. For the algebraic control $R_{01}=e^0\wedge e^1+2e^2\wedge e^3$, $R_{10}=-R_{01}$, with all other entries zero, $\mathcal P=8$ and $P_{\mathrm{all}}=16$. This is an array-normalization control, not a background solution.
