\begin{abstract}
We evaluate the Einstein--Cartan (EC) action with a constant complete Nieh--Yan (NY)
coupling and an irreducible EC-Weyl-squared term on static homogeneous families of
$S^3\times S^1$, $T^3\times S^1$, and a fixed compact
$\mathrm{Nil}^3\times S^1$. Varying radius and volume-preserving shape at fixed
torsion parameters and circle circumference, we classify boundedness and
attainment of minima of the reduced potential. The complete NY density and
the form-index Pontryagin density vanish for the prescribed connection,
while the Weyl contribution is governed by the spatial shape dependence.
We prove that the irreducible EC-Weyl tensor equals the Levi-Civita (LC) Weyl tensor
throughout these families, so their reduced potentials and classifications
coincide. A brief comparison isolates the additional curvature sectors in
a different Ricci-tracefree quadratic action.
For $S^3$ at nonpositive Weyl coupling, an exact factorization of the shape
difference reduces global minimization in two variables to the round slice.
A unique finite-radius minimum exists for $V\ne0$ and $\eta^2<4$; its radius
and value are independent of the Weyl coupling. The remaining parameter
regions distinguish unattained infima, a degenerate minimum set, and a
large-radius runaway. Positive coupling produces small-radius unboundedness
for nonround spheres and Nil. Comparison with the shape-independent torus
potential and the monotone Nil shape dependence identifies how geometry
and variation paths determine the extrema. These results concern the
specified static homogeneous reductions with the stated fixed data.
\end{abstract}
